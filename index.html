Linear Equation Solving Rules

GCSE Higher
1. Unknowns on Both Sides \(ax + b = cx + d\)
\(3(2x + 5) - 2(x - 1) = 3(x + 8) \implies x = 7\)

Strategy: Expand all brackets, collect like terms on each side, then subtract the smaller \(x\)-term across.

2. Algebraic Fractions \(\text{LCM of Denominators}\)
\(\frac{2x + 1}{3} - \frac{x - 3}{4} = 4 \implies 4(2x+1) - 3(x-3) = 48\)

Clear Fractions: Multiply every single term by the LCM of all denominators to remove fractions.

3. Geometric & Word Models Perimeters / Ratios
\(\text{Sum of Angles} = 180^\circ \text{ or } \text{Perimeter} = \sum \text{sides}\)

Formulate & Solve: Set up an algebraic statement from geometric rules or word constraints and isolate the variable.
